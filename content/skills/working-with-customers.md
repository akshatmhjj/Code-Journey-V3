## In plain English

Customers describe solutions ("we need a dashboard"); good engineers dig for the problem underneath ("we can't tell which orders will be late"). Working with customers is the skill of asking the right questions, building the smallest thing that helps, and being honest about what's possible.

## Why it matters

For forward deployed and solutions roles it's half the job. For every other engineer, it's what turns a good coder into someone a business trusts with its hardest problems.
