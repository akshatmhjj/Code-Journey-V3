## What matters first

Projects show you can build. That’s what gets attention early. Algorithms don’t matter if you can’t create working applications.

## Where DSA fits

DSA matters later when you optimize or prepare for specific interviews. Without context, it feels abstract and disconnected.

## Right sequence

Build first, understand fundamentals, then learn DSA. This order makes everything easier and more meaningful.
