## What they do

Window functions let you calculate values across rows without collapsing them. That means you keep full datasets while still analyzing patterns inside them.

## Why they matter

Most real analysis involves comparisons - previous values, rankings, trends. Window functions make those patterns easy to express in a single query.

## How to learn

Start with LAG and LEAD. Once you understand how data flows across rows, everything else becomes easier. It’s less about syntax and more about thinking in sequences.
