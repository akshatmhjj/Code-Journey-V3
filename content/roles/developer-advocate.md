## Is this role for you?

DevRel suits developers who love teaching, writing and community - who get as much satisfaction from someone else's "it works!" as their own. You need to stay hands-on: credibility with developers comes from real technical skill.

## How to start

Start in public now. Write about what you learn, answer questions in communities, and contribute to docs. That body of work is your portfolio - many advocates were hired because a company noticed it.
