## Is this role for you?

Solutions engineering suits technically strong people who enjoy talking to customers, explaining things clearly and solving a new problem every week. You'll write less production code than a developer, but you need enough depth to be credible with engineers.

## Solutions Engineer or FDE?

Solutions engineers focus on designing and demonstrating solutions, often before a sale. [Forward Deployed Engineers](/roles/forward-deployed-engineer) write much more code and build inside the customer's environment after the sale.
