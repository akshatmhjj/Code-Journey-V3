## In plain English

Data structures are containers with different strengths: a hash map finds things instantly, a sorted array lets you search by halving, a tree keeps things ordered as they change. Algorithms are recipes that use them. Choosing the right pair turns a program that takes hours into one that takes milliseconds.

## Why it matters

Most software interviews — especially at larger companies — include one or two DSA problems. Beyond interviews, it trains you to notice when code will fall over as data grows.

## How to practise

Learn **patterns**, not individual problems. Work through a curated list (like NeetCode's) by topic, explain your approach out loud, and revisit problems you couldn't solve a week later. Quality beats quantity.
